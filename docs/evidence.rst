Model Evidence Boundary
-----------------------

The evidence pack separates:

* open online model evidence with R2 about 0.90, including metrics,
  predictions, and a scatter plot;
* proprietary/offline benchmark evidence with R2 about 0.96, marked as
  proprietary/external benchmark evidence rather than open reusable model
  evidence.

Human Authority
---------------

ROBIN provides monitoring, deviation detection, and AI-assisted recommendations.
These recommendations are advisory: the human operator remains the final
authority for process decisions and can accept, reject, override, or adjust
recommendations before action is taken.
